Help & FAQs

Here are some common questions and answers:

  • Q: How do I create a certificate? A: Go to the Create Certificate page, fill in the required fields, and click Generate.
  • Q: Where can I find my issued certificates? A: All your created certificates are stored in the My Certificates page.
  • Q: Can I change my profile details? A: Yes, via the My Account sidebar where you can change your username, password, and profile picture.
  • Q: How do I log out? A: Logout from the settings or account options available in the site.

Still have questions? Visit our Contact page to get in touch.
